My first fiction in a long time, and it was such a delight. Approachable even if you don't have a science background — witty, likeable, and an exhilarating ride as he sets out to save humanity and then some. I loved the science of it, and cruised through because it was so hard to put down.

I read the book before watching the movie, and I enjoyed the book far more. A tip: give it a few months after reading before you watch the film, if you don't want to be disappointed.

Highly recommend.
